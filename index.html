Worland Dog Law

Every provision in the municipal code that governs owning a dog, gathered out of scattered articles and set in one clear space.

Scope & Overview

All twenty-five chapters of the code were checked. The word “dog” appears in no chapter outside Chapter 4. Every rule below is therefore drawn from Chapter 4 — Animals and Fowl — with two provisions pulled in by cross-reference.

Worth knowing: Chapter 14A is the Noise chapter, which includes distinct provisions regarding persistent animal noises and howling disturbances.
